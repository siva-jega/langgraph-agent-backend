# Support Knowledge Base

## Password Reset

Users can reset their password from the login page by selecting "Forgot password". They will receive an email with a reset link. Reset links expire after 30 minutes.

## Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are returned to the original payment method and usually take 5 to 10 business days.

## Shipping Times

Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping usually takes 1 to 2 business days.

## Account Deletion

Users can delete their account from Settings. Account deletion permanently removes profile data after a 7 day grace period.
